# Recording guide (give the relevant lines to the player)

**Best single angle:** behind the player, slightly to the hitting-arm side, camera at waist-to-chest height,
far enough back that the whole body including feet and the racket at full reach stay in frame.

**What each angle shows**

| Angle | Good for | Hides |
|---|---|---|
| Behind (back fence) | Unit turn, swing path, follow-through, spacing sideways, serve toss left/right | How far in front contact is, knee bend depth |
| Side (level with the player, facing them from the hitting side) | Contact point front/back, knee bend, weight transfer, serve trophy position and toss depth | Rotation, sideways spacing |
| Front (from the net) | Racket face, grip, split step | Depth of everything |

For a full picture: 1-2 minutes from behind plus 1-2 minutes from the side.

**Settings**
- Phone on a tripod, fence mount or propped firmly. A hand-held or panning camera ruins the tracking.
- Landscape, 1080p. 60 fps if available (the racket moves ~40 cm between frames at 30 fps); slow-motion 120-240 fps
  is excellent for serves.
- Sun behind the camera. Avoid shooting into the sun or deep shade.
- Keep sound on: the impact sound is used to find the exact contact frame.
- One player in frame if possible; say which one is you if not.

**Content**
- 1-3 minutes is plenty; 8-10 repetitions of each stroke you want rated.
- Hit normally - the swing you really use, not your best-behaviour swing.
- Serves: record 8-10 on their own.
- Uploading: if the file is too large for the chat app, trim it or export at 720p - quality at 720p is enough.

**For progress tracking:** same angle, same distance, similar drill, 2-4 weeks apart, and send the earlier
`tennis-progress.json` (or the earlier report) with the new video.
